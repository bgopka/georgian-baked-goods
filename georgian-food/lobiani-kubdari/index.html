Georgian food, explained

Lobiani, kubdari and chebureki: Georgia's filled breads

In shortLobiani is a Georgian bread filled with seasoned, mashed red beans. Its name comes from lobio, Georgian for beans. Kubdari is a bread from the mountain region of Svaneti, filled with hand-chopped spiced meat and onion. Both are baked like khachapuri, but with a different filling.

Lobiani: the bean bread

Beans are everyday food in Georgia, and lobiani turns them into something you can carry. Red kidney beans are cooked soft, mashed and seasoned, often with onion, herbs and spices, then sealed inside a round of bread and baked.

Lobiani is especially linked with Racha, a mountain region in western Georgia, where it is often made with ham (Rachuli lobiani). Across Georgia it is also the traditional dish of Barbaroba, the feast of Saint Barbara on December 17.

We make it two ways: as a soft round bread, and as Penovani Lobiani, folded in flaky, puff-style pastry.

On our menu: #21 Penovani Lobiani, #22 Lobiani Large and #23 Lobiani Medium

Kubdari: the meat bread of Svaneti

Svaneti sits high in the Caucasus in northwest Georgia, a region of stone defence towers and long winters. Its signature bread is kubdari: a round of dough packed with meat that is chopped by knife rather than ground, mixed with onion and spices, and baked until the juices soak into the crust.

Svan cooks are known for their spice blends, including the famous Svan salt, a mix of salt, garlic and herbs. Kubdari is hearty enough to be a meal on its own.

On our menu: #24 Kubdari

Chebureki: a guest from Crimea

Chebureki are not Georgian in origin. They come from the Crimean Tatar kitchen: a thin dough folded into a half-moon around seasoned meat and fried until crisp and blistered. They became loved across the Black Sea region and the former Soviet Union, including Georgia, which is why you will find them in Georgian bakeries around the world, ours included.

Common questions

Is lobiani vegetarian?

The traditional filling is beans and seasoning. Some regional versions, like Rachuli lobiani, add ham. Ask us about our recipe if you need to be sure.

What does kubdari taste like?

Like a meat pie with more spice and texture: knife-chopped meat, onion and Svan spices, with the juices baked into the crust.

When is lobiani traditionally eaten?

Any day, but especially on Barbaroba, the feast of Saint Barbara on December 17.

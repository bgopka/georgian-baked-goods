Georgian food, explained

Georgia, the country in the Caucasus between the Black Sea and the Caspian, has one of the world's great bread cultures. These guides explain the dishes we bake at Georgian Baked Goods in Etobicoke: where each one comes from, what is inside, and how Georgians eat it.

Our bakery

A little corner of Georgia, baked fresh every morning.

In Georgia, bread isn't a side dish — it's the centre of the table. At Georgian Baked Goods, Valeriy bakes the recipes he grew up with: dough made by hand, stretched and shaped every day, filled with salty sulguni and imeruli cheese, seasoned beans, or spiced meat.

Call ahead for a warm khachapuri, take home a Megruli for the table, or pick up khinkali for dinner. Everything is made here, in small batches, from morning till close.

Questions

Where is Georgian Baked Goods?

At 10 Plastics Ave., Unit 100, Etobicoke, Toronto, ON M8Z 4B7. Look for the black-and-gold sign at number 10.

What are your hours?

Open Tuesday to Sunday, 11 am to 8 pm. Closed on Mondays.

How do I order?

Call 647 479 8929 or order online at georgianbakedgoods.com/order.html, then pick up at the bakery. You pay when you pick up. Walk-ins are welcome too.

Do you take large orders or catering?

Yes. Large orders and catering are welcome: call us or email sales@georgianbakedgoods.com with the date and quantities.

What is khachapuri?

Georgia's national cheese bread: dough wrapped around salty Georgian cheese. We bake six kinds, including round Imeruli, cheese-topped Megruli and boat-shaped Adjaruli with butter and egg yolk.

Do you have meat-free options?

Yes. The khachapuri fillings are cheese (with egg in Adjaruli and Gurulo), and lobiani is filled with seasoned red beans. Kubdari, khinkali, chebureki and Lily contain meat.

Is everything made on site?

Yes. Everything is made by hand at the bakery, in small batches, from morning till close.

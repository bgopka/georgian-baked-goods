Our bakery

A little corner of Georgia, baked fresh every morning.

In Georgia, bread isn't a side dish — it's the centre of the table. At Georgian Baked Goods, Valeriy bakes the recipes he grew up with: dough made by hand, stretched and shaped every day, filled with salty sulguni and imeruli cheese, seasoned beans, or spiced meat.

Call ahead for a warm khachapuri, take home a Megruli for the table, or pick up khinkali for dinner. Everything is made here, in small batches, from morning till close.
